# What you still control

## Always yours

- Your **seed phrase** and wallet password
- The choice to connect or disconnect
- Whether to create a will, recovery plan, or neither
- Edits to the will and recovery plan **before** inheritance starts
- Everyday vault actions (within the rules you published)

## Optional helpers

- **Executors** only receive the permissions you grant
- **Email notifications** are opt-in and verifiable; they remind people, they do not move funds by themselves
- **Find vaults** helps you rediscover vaults linked to your wallet after you sign a message — it does not give GenWealth custody

## What GenWealth does not do

- Hold your seed phrase
- Bypass the Cardano rules once they are on-chain
- Turn off inheritance after it has started
- Replace legal advice in your country — GenWealth is a technical tool; talk to a professional for legal compliance

{% hint style="info" %}
Self-custody with recovery and inheritance is the product promise. Optional services (assisted claiming for non-technical heirs, firm tooling) may sit on top later; they are described as services or future work in the whitepaper, not as requirements to use the vault.
{% endhint %}
