# Overview

GenWealth gives you a **vault** on Cardano — a smart wallet you control with your everyday Cardano wallet.

On top of holding and sending assets, the vault can enforce rules you choose:

- **Blockchain will** — after a quiet period (proof of life), beneficiaries can claim according to shares and reserved assets. Optional executors help with limited permissions.
- **Recovery plan** — after a (usually shorter) quiet period, co-signers can help set a new owner wallet if you lose the old one.
- **Everyday tools** — staking, fractionalization, and Minswap, still under the same vault control.

Nothing here requires GenWealth to hold your seed phrase. The blockchain enforces the rules once you publish them. GenWealth’s app and services help you build actions, discover vaults, and send optional email reminders.
