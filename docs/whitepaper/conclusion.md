# Conclusion

You can see the risk we all face due to the lack of practical on-chain recovery and inheritance. This is a risk for everyone who believes in crypto and holds it long-term in wallets only they control.

It also raises a fundamental question: how can cryptocurrency be a lasting alternative store of value if we cannot pass it to the next generation — or recover it ourselves — in a safe, secure, and trust-minimized way?

!!! warning
    There is a significant long-term risk stemming from the absence of reliable, on-chain recovery and inheritance mechanisms for privately controlled crypto assets.


The next chapters describe GenWealth’s solution. For current product steps, also see [How GenWealth works](../how-it-works/overview.md) and the [user guides](../find-a-guide.md).
