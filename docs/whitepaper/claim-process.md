# Claim process for beneficiaries

Beneficiaries should have a straightforward path to claim. GenWealth provides guides and community channels for people who are new to wallets.

!!! info
    Owners can choose how beneficiaries are notified — for example email when they are added, when assets become claimable, or by handling notifications themselves. See [Contacts and email notifications](../guides/contacts-email.md).


1. **Connect wallet**

    Beneficiaries connect **their own** wallet to the app — the address that was listed in the will.

2. **See inheritances**

    Once connected, they can find vaults where they are named (including **Find vaults** or import by vault ID). They may see that they are a beneficiary before exact claimable amounts unlock.

3. **Claim in two stages**

    When the vault is in inheritance mode and waits have passed:
    
    1. Claim **specific assets** reserved for them.
    2. Claim their **share** of what remains.
    
    Each step is a wallet approval that sends assets to their address.


Full product steps: [Claim your inheritance](../guides/claim-inheritance.md).
