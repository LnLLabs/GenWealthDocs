# What you need to know about Cardano

This page covers only what you need before you use GenWealth. You do not need to become a blockchain expert.

## Cardano and ADA

**Cardano** is the blockchain GenWealth runs on. When you create a vault, set a will, or claim assets, those actions are recorded on Cardano.

**ADA** is Cardano’s coin. You use a little ADA to pay network fees when you approve an action. You can also hold ADA and other Cardano assets inside your GenWealth vault.

## What a wallet is

A **wallet** is an app (usually in your browser) that holds your keys and lets you approve actions. GenWealth asks your wallet to connect and to confirm each important step. The wallet is how GenWealth knows it is really you.

Common wallets GenWealth can detect include **Eternl**, **Lace**, and **Vespr**. You can also use **NuFi** social login if you prefer to sign in that way.

## Seed phrase

A **seed phrase** (sometimes called a recovery phrase) is a secret list of words created when you set up a wallet. Anyone who has those words can move your money.

!!! warning
    Write your seed phrase on paper and store it offline. Never type it into GenWealth, an email, a chat, or any website. GenWealth will never ask for it.


## What GenWealth controls — and what it does not

- **You** keep the wallet and the seed phrase.
- **Your connected wallet** stays in control of the GenWealth vault.
- **GenWealth** provides the screens and builds the actions you approve. It does not take custody of your assets.
