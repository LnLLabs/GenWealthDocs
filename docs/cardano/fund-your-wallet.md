# Fund your wallet

You need a small amount of **ADA** in your wallet to pay network fees and to create a GenWealth vault. Extra ADA can later be moved into the vault.

1. **Open your wallet’s receive screen**

    In your wallet app, open **Receive** (or the equivalent). You will see a long address and usually a QR code.

2. **Copy your receive address carefully**

    Copy the full address. Send a tiny test amount first if you are moving a large sum from an exchange.

3. **Send ADA to that address**

    Buy ADA on an exchange and withdraw to your wallet address, or ask someone to send you ADA. Wait until the balance appears in your wallet before you continue.

4. **Keep a little ADA free for fees**

    Every GenWealth action you approve costs a small network fee. Leave some ADA in the connected wallet (not only in the vault) so you can still approve the next step.


## Testing on preprod

GenWealth may run on Cardano **preprod** (a public test network). Test ADA has no real-world value. You get it from a **faucet** for that network, not from a normal exchange. Switch your wallet to the matching network before you request test ADA.

!!! info
    Mainnet availability depends on GenWealth’s public release. Follow the in-app network and the announcements on [genwealth.app](https://genwealth.app/). This documentation describes the product as it works on the live preprod deployment.
