# Create a Cardano wallet

You need a Cardano wallet before you can open GenWealth. This guide walks through a simple, safe setup.

1. **Choose a wallet GenWealth can detect**

    Install a browser wallet such as **Eternl**, **Lace**, or **Vespr** from the wallet’s official website or your browser’s extension store. You can also use **NuFi** if you prefer social login later in GenWealth.
    
    Use only the official download. Bookmark the real site so you do not land on a lookalike.

2. **Create a new wallet**

    Open the wallet extension and choose **Create new wallet** (wording may vary slightly). The wallet will show a **seed phrase** — a secret list of words.

3. **Write the seed phrase down offline**

    Copy the words onto paper, in order. Store that paper somewhere safe that only you can reach. Do not take a screenshot or store the phrase in cloud notes, email, or chat.

4. **Confirm you wrote it correctly**

    Most wallets ask you to re-enter a few words. Complete that check so you know your paper copy is accurate.

5. **Set a wallet password**

    Choose a strong password for opening the wallet on this device. The password unlocks the app on your computer; it is separate from the seed phrase. If you forget the password, you restore the wallet with the seed phrase.


!!! warning
    If someone else gets your seed phrase, they can move your assets. Treat it like the keys to a safe.


!!! info
    This documentation does not rank wallets. Pick one GenWealth lists on the connect screen so connection works smoothly.
