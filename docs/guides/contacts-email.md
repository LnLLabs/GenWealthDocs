# Contacts and email notifications

Open **Contacts** to keep local address book entries and to manage **Email notifications**.

## Contacts

Contacts are stored for your convenience in the app (for example to reuse addresses when building a will). You can export or import them; treat export files as sensitive.

From a contact, when a will or recovery plan exists, shortcuts may include **Send Assets**, **Add to Will**, **Add Executor**, or **Recovery Contact**.

## Email notifications

1. **Register an email**

    In the email panel, **Register** (or **Re-register**) your address. GenWealth uses a privacy-preserving commitment on-chain; it does not put your raw email on the public blockchain.

2. **Verify the link**

    Open the magic link GenWealth sends (the **Verify** page). Until you verify, delivery may stay limited.

3. **Choose what you want to hear about**

    After verification, set preferences such as:
    
    - Immediate
    - When assets are claimable
    - When the wallet enters inheritance mode
    - When the will changes
    - Inheritance approaching
    - Executor changes
    - Owner transfer
    
    You can **Unregister** later if you no longer want email.


!!! info
    Email helps people notice deadlines and claim windows. It does not replace proof of life or on-chain rules.
