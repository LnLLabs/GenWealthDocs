# CTO — Leandros Holleman

Leandros is an open-source developer with 9 years of experience. He has extensive experience in DevOps and cloud solutions, and years of experience as a full-stack Cardano developer. He has contributed to Cardano not only by operating a stake pool, but also by launching dApps on mainnet and pioneering smart-wallet and multisig technologies.

Significant contributions include:

- Years of blockchain development, full-stack across front-end and back-end
- Lead developer for AnetaBTC V2, a major wrapped BTC dApp on Cardano
- Developed KeyPact (Broclan) Multisig Wallet on Cardano — among the early user-friendly multi-signature wallets on Cardano
- Pioneered smart-wallet functionalities on Cardano, enabling self-custodial programmable wallets via smart contracts — technology used in KeyPact and in GenWealth
- Operates the BSP Cardano stake pool, with a large delegator community and tens of millions of ADA staked
