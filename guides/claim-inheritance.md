# Claim your inheritance

If someone named you in a GenWealth blockchain will, you claim from the **Claim Inheritance** screens using **your own** wallet. You never need the owner’s seed phrase.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Connect your wallet

Open GenWealth and connect the wallet whose address was written into the will.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Open Inheritance

From the vault hub, choose **Inheritance**, or open **Claim Inheritance** if you already have a link.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Find or import the vault

If nothing appears, use **Find vaults** (sign a short message with your wallet) or **import by vault ID** if the owner shared the vault ID with you.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Wait until claiming is allowed

Cards show your status, such as waiting for inheritance to start, **Step 1: Specific assets**, **Waiting for other inheritors**, or **Step 2: Claim your share**. Claiming unlocks only after the vault enters inheritance mode and any grace period you were given has passed.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Claim stage 1 — specific assets

When available, choose **Claim Specific Assets** / **Confirm Claim** and approve in your wallet. This collects assets reserved for you personally.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Claim stage 2 — your share

When stage 2 opens, choose **Claim Share** and approve. You receive your percentage of the remaining vault (after fees shown in the confirmation).
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="info" %}
You may see that you are a beneficiary before exact amounts are claimable. Exact payouts appear when each claim step unlocks.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
If a card says the vault inventory is blocked, the vault may not yet hold the reserved assets. Contact the estate’s helper or wait until assets are in the vault.
{% endhint %}

More detail: [Inheritance phases](../how-it-works/inheritance-phases.md).
