# Use your vault wallet

The **Vault Wallet** is where assets in your vault live day to day. Open it from the sidebar after you enter a vault.

## What you can do

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | See balances in the vault |
| **Receive** | Show the vault address so others can send assets to it |
| **Deposit** | Move assets from your connected wallet into the vault |
| **Send** | Send assets from the vault to an address you choose |
| **Stake** | Stake ADA held in the vault (when available on your network) |
| **History** | Review past vault activity |

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Deposit or receive

To fund the vault from the wallet you connected, use **Deposit**. To receive from somewhere else, open **Receive**, copy the vault address, and send to it. Wait until the balance shows before you rely on it.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Send carefully

Choose **Send**, enter the destination address and amount, then confirm. For a full send, you may see **Confirm Send All** and a checkbox to acknowledge. Always double-check the address.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Approve in your wallet

Every send or deposit is approved in your connected Cardano wallet. Cancel in the wallet if something looks wrong.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="info" %}
The banner can show your vault ID. Keep an offline copy. GenWealth cannot recover a lost vault ID for you.
{% endhint %}

Fees for each action appear in the confirmation before you sign. Next you may want to [set up a blockchain will](blockchain-will.md) or a [recovery plan](recovery-plan.md).
