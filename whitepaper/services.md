# Services on top of the solution

On top of the core vault, we are developing complementary services that use GenWealth in the background — especially for transferring crypto to people who are not yet comfortable with wallets.

One identified problem is helping non-technical beneficiaries. A service can:

- Use the dApp for secure vault custody and beneficiary assignment while the owner is alive
- Let technical beneficiaries claim directly in the app
- Offer assisted help for others: guidance to create a wallet and claim, and optionally help sell assets and move value to a bank account when requested

Planned modes:

- **Educational** — support and guidance (for example video call) through the claim process
- **Full-service** — GenWealth acts as an assistant for claim execution on the beneficiary’s behalf under clear contracts

## Liability and guarantees (service design)

The full-service offering is designed with a strong accountability clause: if GenWealth fails to deliver its contracted part and the beneficiary does not receive the value they are entitled to under that service, GenWealth would be liable for **twice** that entitled value. This is meant to align incentives with correct execution.

{% hint style="info" %}
These assisted services are optional and contractual. They are not required to use self-custodial vaults, wills, or recovery in the app. Availability and exact terms will be published when each service launches.
{% endhint %}

Other services may come from GenWealth or from accredited inheritance professionals in different regions.
