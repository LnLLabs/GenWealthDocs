# The solution

With GenWealth, our focus is to solve asset recovery and inheritance by leveraging the programmability of the Cardano blockchain to create **self-custody vaults** (smart wallets). Smart contracts give you more control over your assets and let you publish rules for inheritance and crypto recovery — including when you lose a seed phrase.

The solution is self-custodial, does not require handing intermediaries your keys, and aims for a clear user experience. We work with inheritance professionals so users can optionally involve lawyers and executors with **limited permissions**, without giving them unchecked control of assets.

More than “just Web3,” this approach can reduce cost and time versus purely manual estates, make wills easier to update while you are alive, and reduce certain kinds of fraud through transparent, immutable records.
