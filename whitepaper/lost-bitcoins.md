# How many bitcoins are lost forever?

Industry estimates (for example research popularized by Chainalysis) have long suggested that on the order of **around 20%** of Bitcoin’s supply may be lost forever — seed phrases misplaced, holders deceased without a recovery path, and similar cases.

At the time an earlier version of this whitepaper was written, Bitcoin was trading near **$100,000**, which implied hundreds of billions of dollars of value stranded under those estimates. Prices move; treat that dollar figure as a snapshot from the original paper, not a live quote. The underlying point remains: a large share of crypto can become permanently inaccessible without recovery and inheritance tools.

The problem is significant enough that competing chains have cooperated in efforts such as the **DeRec Alliance**, formed to explore decentralized recovery — with participants that have included networks such as XRP, Algorand, Hedera, and Cardano.

GenWealth addresses this with an on-chain design: recover access when you lose a seed phrase (through a plan you set in advance), and let family or chosen beneficiaries claim according to rules you publish — without handing a custodian your keys.
