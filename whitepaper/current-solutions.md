# Challenges of current recovery solutions

Before diving deeper into the GenWealth solution, it helps to understand the options cryptocurrency users already try — and their limitations.

## Third-party seed storage

Some services keep your seed phrase and share it with your family when you pass away. That requires trusting intermediaries with unlimited access to your wealth. Risks include breaches, weak processes, rogue employees, government compulsion, and high ongoing cost.

## Sharing a physical location

Storing a seed phrase in a vault and telling family where it is is also risky. Trusted people may share the location, face financial pressure, or simply forget the details years later — especially if they are not crypto-savvy.

Even when access works, heirs still need to set up wallets, find exchanges, sell assets, and move value to a bank. Teaching that process repeatedly is a heavy burden; a paper note is rarely enough.

## Giving up self-custody

Relying on an institution to sell and deliver crypto removes seed-phrase management but reintroduces custodian risk. The industry has seen losses from mistakes and attacks. Choosing this route means losing the benefits of self-custody.

GenWealth’s approach is different: keep self-custody, publish rules on-chain, and let recovery and inheritance run without handing someone your seed phrase.
